About

I am a Founding Engineer working on surgical autonomy at Inner Logic. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University in 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Mathias Unberath in the ARCADE Lab, and my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University in 2018.

My research focuses on robust perception and embodied intelligence for surgical robotics. I build digital twin–based scene representations that separate reliable low-level perception from high-level surgical analysis and automation, and benchmarks that test how well these systems hold up under real operating-room conditions such as smoke, bleeding, and poor lighting.

Outside of work, I play soccer. I captained the Johns Hopkins University Chinese Football Club from 2021 to 2024, and I am a Bayern Munich fan.
